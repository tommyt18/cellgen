Simulated 4D microscopy of cells that deform, divide, fuse and die

cellgen is a config-driven simulator that turns a phenomenon description into time-lapse fluorescence z-stacks with exact ground truth: every voxel label, every track, every division, fusion and death event. This page shows each of the eleven simulated systems.

The eleven systems

Each system is one preset: a cell line, a scene, and the phenomenon it is built to show. Pick one to jump to its videos.

Reading the videos

Every video is a maximum-intensity projection along z of the full 3D stack, one frame per time point.

Left: rawWhat the simulated microscope records: blurred by the point-spread function, bleached, with photon and read noise.
Right: cleanThe same acquisition without noise. It ships with every sequence as a denoising target.
Magenta: nucleusChromatin marker (H2B-like). It brightens when chromatin condenses in mitosis and apoptosis.
Green: cytoplasmCytoplasm with an actin-rich cortex. HL-60 has a nucleus channel only and is shown in grey.

Brightness is scaled once per sequence, so bleaching and density changes stay visible. The three 24-hour viral systems are the exception: their display gain follows the bleaching, as noted under each video.

How a sequence is generated

Four layers, kept separate so that each can be calibrated on its own. One YAML preset drives all of them, and any value can be a distribution sampled per cell or per event.

AgentsEach cell is a state machine: cycle phase, death stage, EMT state, infection. Simple mechanics handle adhesion, repulsion and persistent motility.
RasterizationCells are competing implicit fields on a fine isotropic grid, so they never overlap and contact faces and cleavage furrows emerge. The body is rescaled every frame until its rasterized volume matches the target.
OpticsVectorial confocal or widefield point-spread function, z sampling, photobleaching, Poisson photon noise, read noise and camera offset.
Ground truthCell Tracking Challenge label trees and OME-Zarr volumes, plus per-cell state tables and an event log.

Three difficulty tiers

Every system is generated at three tiers that move cell density, photon budget and z sampling together, the axes that make real tracking data hard.

Perturbation systems (apoptosis, EMT, infection) also include untreated controls, and EMT and SARS-CoV-2 are swept over induction strength and multiplicity of infection.

Fidelity and limits

What has been checked against real data, and what has not.

Calibrated against real data

  • HL-60 against Fluo-N3DH-SIM+ (sim / real medians): nucleus volume 117 / 122 µm³, sphericity 0.77 / 0.80, mitotic brightening 1.23 / 1.23. Background noise is still about 25% high.
  • A549 against Fluo-C3DH-A549: cell volume 1010–1090 / 1155 µm³, sphericity 0.59–0.61 / 0.67. Real A549 centroids move subdiffusively, which the persistent random walk here does not reproduce.

Known limits

  • The other nine systems use literature parameter ranges and are not fitted to a reference dataset.
  • Shapes come from competing implicit fields. There is no membrane mechanics.
  • The point-spread function does not vary with depth.
  • The virus field is two-dimensional, so infection is only modelled in monolayers.
  • The Cell Tracking Challenge format cannot express merges. Cell fusion is recorded in the event log, and fused nuclei keep their own tracks.
